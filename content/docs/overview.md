## What is Meshtastic?

[Meshtastic](https://meshtastic.org/) is a free, open source project that turns small radios into a way to send text messages without cell service or internet access. Think of a walkie-talkie for texting: you write a message on your phone, and your radio sends it over the air.

Nearby radios can automatically pass that message along, forming a mesh that reaches farther than one radio alone. Meshtastic uses LoRa, a long-range, low-power radio technology. It's built for short messages, optional location sharing, and sensor readings, rather than voice calls or internet browsing.

People use it to keep in touch on hikes, coordinate at events, practice for outages, or simply chat with neighbors and experiment with radio. You need a working path between radios, so placement and the nodes around you make a big difference. Our [FAQs]({{< ref "/docs/faq" >}}) cover the basics, and [Getting Started]({{< ref "/docs/getting-started" >}}) walks you through joining.

## Who is BayMesh?

BayMesh began as a community of people building and using Meshtastic across the San Francisco Bay Area. Today, the mesh stretches from Redding in the north to Monterey in the south, and east beyond Placerville into the foothills. We're nearly **4,000 members strong**: radio enthusiasts, tinkerers, neighbors, and people who just want to stay connected—all helping grow a mesh that anyone can join.

We help each other get started, test coverage, find better places for nodes, and keep messages moving around the Bay. We also maintain local guides, maps, and tools, and get together on [Discord](https://discord.gg/GpsKbhGJx5) and for our [weekly on-air check-in]({{< ref "/docs/weekly-net" >}}).

You don't need a technical background or a fancy radio to participate. Bring your curiosity, ask questions, and help someone else when you can. Every useful node and friendly reply helps the community grow.

The world can be divisive. Here, we leave politics at the door—we're all one big happy mesh.
