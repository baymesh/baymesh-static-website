## Other regional meshes

These community guides offer useful examples of setup advice, troubleshooting, and local mesh projects. When joining BayMesh, use our [recommended settings]({{< ref "/docs/getting-started/recommended-settings" >}}); another region's preset or frequency slot may be different.

- [Austin Mesh](https://www.austinmesh.org/join/meshtastic-setup/): A step-by-step guide to setting up a node.
- [MeshOregon](https://meshoregon.com/meshtastic-alpha-club/): An example of a group coordinating separate settings for experiments.
- [SoCal Mesh](https://socalmesh.org/help/best-practices/): Placement, device roles, and ways to reduce unnecessary mesh traffic.
- [South City Mesh](https://www.southcitymesh.com/faq): Nearby community advice on getting connected and understanding coverage.
