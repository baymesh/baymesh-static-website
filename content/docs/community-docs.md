## Meshtastic and downloads

* [Meshtastic.org](https://meshtastic.org/) — The official Meshtastic website.
* [All downloads](https://meshtastic.org/downloads/) — Find Meshtastic apps and firmware.
* [Web Flasher](https://flasher.meshtastic.org/) — Install firmware on supported radios from your browser.
* [iOS app](https://itunes.apple.com/WebObjects/MZStore.woa/wa/viewSoftware?id=1586432531) — Meshtastic for Apple devices.
* [Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.geeksville.mesh) — Meshtastic on Google Play.
* [Firmware on GitHub](https://github.com/meshtastic/firmware) — Source code and firmware releases.
* [Donate to Meshtastic](https://opencollective.com/meshtastic) — Support the project and its development through Open Collective.

## Hardware and community guides

* [Meshware](https://meshware.net/) — Compare Meshtastic hardware, features, and specifications to find your next node.
* [awesome-meshtastic](https://github.com/ShakataGaNai/awesome-meshtastic/) — A curated collection of Meshtastic guides, hardware, software, maps, and projects.
